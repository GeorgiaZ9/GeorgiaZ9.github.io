01 / ABOUT

About me

I’m interested in cosmology, statistical methods, and writing software for physics research.

For my MSc thesis, I’m developing mock fast radio burst catalogues and studying their correlations with galaxies. I use Bayesian inference to estimate cosmological parameters from these simulated data.

Previously, I participated in the development of scientific software at CERN for the Muon Collider project, contributing the material-properties library used by the team’s simulation tools. I also worked on theoretical high-energy physics at NCSR Demokritos. Alongside my studies, I work as a Data & AI Engineer at Munich Re.

02 / RESEARCH

Research

CURRENT MSc THESIS01

Fast radio bursts &
cosmological inference

Generating mock FRB redshifts and dispersion measures, including large-scale structure and host-galaxy contributions.

  • Likelihoods, priors, and MCMC with emcee to infer H0 and Ωm.
  • FRB–galaxy cross-correlations with the Landy–Szalay estimator.
  • Comparing shared and independent halo populations across mock realizations.

SMEFT & scattering amplitudes

Symbolic SMEFT calculations and a top-philic Z′ model in HELAC-PHEGAS, compared with the Standard Model at leading order.
